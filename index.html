FAQ

Which watches are supported?

Garmin works in the beta. Apple Watch and Amazfit are next, Wear OS after that.

Does my client need to pay?

No. The app is free for clients, always.

Does it drain the battery?

Coach Link only sends data during a session you have started together, so a normal workout is not affected noticeably. We will publish real numbers from the beta.

How many clients can I track at once?

Up to 5 on PRO, unlimited on PRO MAX.

Is client data private?

Clients choose what to share and can stop anytime. See our privacy policy.

When does the beta start?

The beta starts on December 1. We review applications and reply within 7 days, so apply now to get a founding-coach spot.
