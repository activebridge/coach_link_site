Privacy policy

What we collect

The details you enter in the beta application or launch-event form: name, email, social handle, location, coaching type, number of clients and watch types. We also record which channel you came from (UTM tags) and a referral code if you used one.

Why

To review beta applications, contact you about the beta and the launch event, and decide which watches to support first.

Who sees it

Only the Coach Link team and the services we use to store the data and send email. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

Write to hello@coachlink.app to see, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent at any time.

Health data

This site does not collect health data. The Coach Link app only shares what a client chooses to share with their coach.
